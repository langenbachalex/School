*Station 1*

- A9. Through the window, you saw a black car. Waiting.
- A10. "We have to go," you said. "Now."

**Gabelung 1 – oben (B): weiterfahren**
- B6. Lucas didn't want to stop. "Keep driving. Please."

- B8. A black car appeared in the mirror. Then another.
- B9. Jayden drove faster. The old car started to shake.
- B10. "Hold on!" he shouted. "This is going to be fun!"
