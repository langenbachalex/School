**Sprache in diesem Kapitel:** KERN Ortsangaben als Wendungen (on the wall, under the bench, next to the door, on the shelf, above the fireplace, behind the clock, between the cups, in the corner, at the window, down the stairs) · there is / there are · Uhrzeit (quarter past nine, twenty past nine, half past nine, 9:53). WIEDERHOLT: Häufigkeit (never, always, sometimes), he/she/it + s, do/does mit Kurzantwort (Zeugen-Probe), Adjektive (old, warm, strange, black). DARÜBER HINAUS: present progressive in drei Sätzen (is running, is eating, is sitting) als Vorgriff auf Kapitel 2.

**Was man im Raum tun kann:** frei umsehen (Raum lässt sich schieben), alles antippen, mit Fuchs und Taube reden, Dinge mitnehmen. Jede englische Zeile lässt sich antippen und zeigt Deutsch. Es gibt keine Aufgaben-Überschriften; die Namen der Abschnitte unten sind nur für uns.

## A. Ankunft (Erzähler Mr. Alex; wörtliche Rede von den Figuren)
1. Tower of London. Saturday night, quarter past nine.
2. Sergeant Bearskin never runs. Tonight he is running.
3. "The keys are gone!" he shouts. "Mr Pike is gone too!"
4. Toni looks at you. „Was sagt er?“
5. "At 9:53 we lock the Tower," says the Sergeant.
6. "Every night. For seven hundred years. We are never late."
7. "No keys, no ceremony," says the fox. He is eating a sandwich.
8. "Where is my hat?" – "On your head, Sergeant."
9. "Good. Here is your badge, detective. You have thirty-eight minutes."
10. He runs out. The door closes. Something moves at the window.

*Du bekommst das Abzeichen „Junior Detective“ (erstes Teil für deine Figur).*

## C. Der Fuchs (antippen, Reihenfolge frei)
- F1. "I never take keys. I sometimes take sandwiches."
- F2. "Mr Pike is always here at nine. He is never late."
- F3. "Tonight he is not here. That is strange."
- F4. "Ask the pigeon. She sees everything. She never stops talking."

**Tonis False Friend:** Toni versucht es selbst: "I become a sandwich?" – Fox: "You become a sandwich? I want to see that." – Toni: „Ich wollte doch nur eins haben!“ Du hilfst: *"Can I have a sandwich?"* (Auswahl aus drei Sätzen; der Fuchs gibt eins ab, widerwillig.)

## D. Die Taube am Fenster (Hören; sie redet schnell und gern)
- P1. "Coo! I sit at this window every night. I see everything."
- P2. "At nine o'clock Mr Pike is in the Guard Room."
- P3. "He has the keys in his hand. And a big bag on his back."
- P4. "A woman is with him. They drink tea. They whisper."
- P5. "At ten past nine he puts something behind the clock."
- P6. "Then they go down the stairs. Down, not out!"
- P7. "And he sings. Mr Pike never sings!"

**Toni fragt nach** („Hat sie das wirklich gesagt?“) – im Spiel als kurzes Hin und Her, drei Antworten: *Yes, she does. · No, she doesn't. · She doesn't say.*
| Toni meint | Richtig |
|---|---|
| Mr Pike has the keys in his hand. | Yes, she does. |
| The bag is on the table. | No, she doesn't. (on his back) |
| The woman is the cook. | She doesn't say. |
| Mr Pike puts something under the clock. | No, she doesn't. (behind the clock) |
| They go down the stairs. | Yes, she does. |

## E. Hinter der Uhr
- E1. There is a note behind the clock.
- E2. "Don't look for the keys. Look for the cake. – P."
- E3. Toni: „Kuchen? Jetzt?“ – "I like this case," says the fox.

## F. Der Rabe auf der Treppe (der Weg nach unten)
- R1. A raven sits on the stairs. He is big and black. He does not move.
- R2. "Where is the note?" he asks. "Tell me, and I move."
*Du antwortest mit einer Karte aus dem Notizbuch. Richtig: **behind the clock**. Bei anderen Karten antwortet der Rabe trocken, z. B. auf „under the bench“: "That is a boot. I am not a boot."*
- R3. "Good," says the raven. His eyes are gold.
- R4. "Seven hooks. Seven ravens. Count us tonight."
- R5. Then he is gone. Not out of the window. Just gone.
- R6. The clock says half past nine. Twenty-three minutes.
- R7. Something smells good down there. Very good.

*Rabe 1 von 7 gefunden: **Nox**. Entscheidung fürs nächste Kapitel: Geht Toni mit dir in die Küche, oder folgt Toni dem Fuchs? (ändert Szene und Witze in Kapitel 2).*

## G. Dein Notizbuch schreibt mit (Lücken mit den gefundenen Karten füllen)
> Case notes, half past nine. The keys are not **on the wall**. There is one boot **under the bench**. The teapot **on the table** is still warm. There is a spoon **between the cups** – two cups! Mr Pike puts a note **behind the clock**. Then he goes **down the stairs**.

**Detective's Notes (freiwillig, eine Seite im Notizbuch):** *There is* one thing. *There are* two things or more.
