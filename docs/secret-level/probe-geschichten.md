## Klasse 6 · Hero File 01 – Leo the Lion and the Missing Hour
**Öffnet sich mit:** Superheld Leo + Ort Big Ben · Niveau: Unit 3 (Tagesablauf, Uhrzeiten, he/she/it + s)

It is **morning** in London. Leo the Lion runs to Big Ben. He looks at the **clock** and he is surprised. It says **ten past nine**, but his watch says **ten past eight**!

"Why is the clock wrong?" Leo asks. He **finds out** that a little bird sits on the big hand. The clock **can't** move.

Leo is super fast, but today he needs a plan. He calls his friend Tessa the Turtle. "Can you **come** to Big Ben?" he asks. "It **takes** me ten minutes," says Tessa. "I am slow, but I always come."

At **8 o'clock** Tessa is there. She is calm. She **shows** the bird a sandwich. The bird flies away, and the clock moves again.

"**Well done**, Tessa!" says Leo. **In the end**, the clock is right, and everybody in London is on time.

**Questions**
1. What does Leo see on the clock? (It says ten past nine.)
2. Why can't the clock move? (A little bird sits on the big hand.)
3. How does Tessa help? (She shows the bird a sandwich / the bird flies away.)

**Wörter:** morning, clock, ten past nine, find out, can't, come, takes, 8 o'clock, show, well done, in the end

## Klasse 9 · Road Trip File 01 – The Last Ferry
**Öffnet sich mit:** Landmark-Karte Statue of Liberty + Staat New York · Niveau: Unit 1–2 (Zeitformen, -ing)

Jeremy and Jessica are standing on Liberty Island. It is getting dark, and the tourists are leaving. "Look at the time!" Jessica says. "The last ferry left ten minutes ago."

Jeremy checks his phone. "No signal. Great." Then he laughs. "Do you remember what the guide said? The statue was a present from France in 1886. Maybe France sends us a boat."

A park ranger is walking along the path. She smiles. "You two look lost," she says. "I'm closing the gate, but a service boat is going to Manhattan in half an hour. You can come with me – if you help me carry these boxes."

On the boat, Jessica is looking back at the statue. The torch is shining against the dark sky. "We missed the ferry," she says, "but we got the best view of the whole trip."

**Questions**
1. Why are Jeremy and Jessica worried? (They missed the last ferry.)
2. What does the park ranger offer? (A ride on the service boat if they carry boxes.)
3. What does Jessica think at the end? (They got the best view of the trip.)

**Wörter:** tourist, leave/left, signal, present, ranger, lost, carry, shine/shining
