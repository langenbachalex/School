Destiny (Jessica): When I started at Lincoln High, everything was intimidating – the hallways, the noise, the seniors. Then I joined the debate team. I'm really good at arguing – just ask my parents! We practise on Mondays and Wednesdays, and next month we're going to a competition in Seattle. I'm a bit nervous about speaking in front of the judges, but I'm really looking forward to it.
Ethan (Jeremy B.): Sports have never really been my thing. I prefer making things, so I joined the stage crew for the school play. We build the sets and do the lights. It's hard work, and before a show we sometimes stay until nine at night. But when the curtain goes up and everything works – that's incredible.
Maya (Jessica): I'm on the volunteering committee. We organize fund-raising events like car washes and bake sales, so that we can help a homeless shelter downtown. Last year we raised as much as three thousand dollars. Sometimes it's hard to find enough volunteers on a Saturday morning. But I love helping people – and it looks great on college applications, too.
Jordan (Jeremy B.): I'm interested in music, so I play the trumpet in the marching band. We play at every football game and at homecoming. Marching and playing at the same time isn't easy, but you get used to it. The best thing is the team spirit – we're like a big family.
