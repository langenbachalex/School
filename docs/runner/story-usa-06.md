## Text
- 1-E1. The gates were closed. But one night, Grandpa's journal began to glow.
- 1-E2. Lucas still had half of the map. On the back, someone had written: TURN THE PAGE.
- 1-E3. When Lucas touched the page CHAPTER 6, the fog came back – and took you all.
2. You woke up in the Realm. Snow, black banners and a wall of ice.
3. The knight was waiting. "You have found all five gates," he said. "Now read."
4. Lucas opened the journal. The first page was dated June 1974.

*Station 1*

5. 'Dear Lucas,' it said. 'If you are reading this, I have failed.'
6. 'Elena and I had been keepers for ten years. We used to ride the old trails together.'
7. 'Then she had found a sixth gate. A gate that should never be opened.'
8. 'Behind it, she believed, everything she had lost would come back.'

*Station 2*

9. 'I had hidden the map before she could take it. Then I had to run.'
10. 'PS: Don't hate her. She only wanted her little brother back.'

*Boss: Elena Cruz an der sechsten Tür (Wort-Frage)*

11. Elena stood at the sixth gate. Her eyes were wet. "He was only twelve," she whispered.
12. Lucas gave her Grandpa's last page. She read it, smiled – and closed the gate forever.
